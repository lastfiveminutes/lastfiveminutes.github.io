The Last Five Minutes

Rewind! Speaking show

“You cannot control every situation, but you can choose how to face it.”

A team game for the live class. Share this screen in Meet or Zoom. Teams take turns: one student speaks, and the class can shout "REWIND!" when they hear a mistake.

Each team has one Rewind token to change a card they don't like.

Set up the teams

  • Rewind! challenge: talk about a topic card for 60 seconds. Every "REWIND!" restarts the clock. Three rewinds and the turn is lost.
  • Hot seat: a student becomes a character and answers the class's questions in 90 seconds.
  • The clock is mandatory. A turn has no points until the clock starts, and it can't be paused.
